# MeshOptimiser v0.30.0 (Kobarid)

Two alpha features: follow Claude as a feed with your model beside it, and make parts spin, swing, shake and travel.

## New

**Claude mode (alpha).** The app steps out of the way and a feed shows what Claude does: what it says, every call it makes with what came back, and every picture it takes, with your model beside it as a rounded card. Open it from the start screen, the Claude badge, the command search, or with ?claude on the address. A model opens in the same view, with a loading card and a "Ready for Claude" card when it is done. Controls in the feed: ready-made jobs, Connected, Ask before edits, Undo and Redo, Stop. The new tool say lets Claude write to you in the feed.

**Animate (alpha).** Make a part or a group spin, swing, shake or travel along a path, with a playbar and loop points. It is a view, not an edit: saving and exporting put every part back, and the turntable video plays it. Claude can set it up with the new tool animate_parts.

**Instances.** Select all instances, Select the original and Make unique.

**Three choices for Claude.** Each kind of change is Allow, Ask first or Block in Settings › Claude.

## Around the app

A Settings window that looks like one, Advanced flatten with before and after drawings, one title bar for the floating windows, rounder corners, a tidier Export window, a coloured shortcuts window, a quit dialog that keeps Don't save apart, and a Scania V8 engine on the start screen. Dragging and resizing windows, docks and the console is lighter, background checks pause in a hidden tab, and Materials thumbnails draw as they scroll into view.

## Fixed

STEP files made of faceted breps open (the 413 MB Mercedes-Benz Arocs among them). Undo and redo on an exploded view put it back together, and a held Ctrl+Z no longer freezes the page. A deleted part follows the exploded view. Instance counts are live. Dragging a material shows the ball, not the system arrow, and Delete in the Materials window deletes materials. The Model mode button is greyed with nothing selected, grid lines are crisp from far away, and Advanced flatten follows the selection.
