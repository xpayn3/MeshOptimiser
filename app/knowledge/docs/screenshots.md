# Screenshots

Save a picture of the viewport as a PNG, at the size of the view or at any resolution up to poster size.

1. Set up the view: orbit to the angle you want and choose a view mode.
2. Open the command search (`Ctrl + K`) and choose **Screenshot (share, copy, download)**, or choose **Save screenshot…** in the menu of the three dots at the right end of the bottom toolbar. The screen flashes like a shutter and the **Save screenshot** window opens.
3. Choose a resolution.
4. Check the file name, or type your own.
5. Press **Save…**. Where the browser allows it, you choose the folder. Otherwise the picture goes to your downloads folder.

## What the options mean

- **Resolution**: Six presets: the viewport at 1×, 2× (sharp) or 4× (poster), and 1080p, 1440p and 4K.
- **Custom (px)**: Any width and height, from 16 to 16,384 pixels a side. The aspect ratio and the number of megapixels are shown beside it.
- **View mode**: **Current** uses the view mode that is on. **Solid**, **Wireframe** and **X-ray** use that mode for the picture only.
- **Hide scene grid**: Leaves the floor lines and the origin axes out of this picture.
- **Filename**: Starts as the model’s name and the time.
- **Stamp info on image**: Prints a small panel in a corner of the picture. Tick what it should show: model name, statistics, selection counts, timestamp, camera, units, and the app’s name. Then pick the corner.

The picture is a PNG file. Right-clicking an empty spot of the viewport also offers **Save screenshot…**, under **More**.

> A 4× picture of a large viewport is a very large file. The limit is 16,384 pixels a side.
