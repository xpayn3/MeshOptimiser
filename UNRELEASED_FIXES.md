# Fixes since the last release

The running log of bugs found and fixed after the last tag. Every fix goes in here the moment it is made (see `CLAUDE.md`), so that
it can be copied into `CHANGELOG.md` and the release notes when the next version ships, and so that there is a record of what was
wrong and why. When a version is released, its entries move into the changelog and this list starts again.

Entry format: the changelog's own (`![fix][fix] **What was wrong, in the user's words.** What now happens.`), written as one person
("I"), never "we". Under each entry, a line for the maintainer: `Cause:` and `Where:` (and `Test:` if one covers it).

Last release in the changelog: **v0.30.0**.

## Fixed

- ![fix][fix] **The cards of the right panel (Small parts, Reduce, Fit to budget, the Shading tiles and others) had heights and positions between pixels.** Their text had a line height of 1.2 to 1.55 times the font (14.4, 17.05, 18.6, 15.95, 21.7 px), and everything under them moved with it; the Fit to budget row took the width of its text. Whole line heights, a fixed width for the budget field, 2 px round the tile pictures. Measured in a browser: the right panel has no box left off the pixel grid at 100 %; four icons in centred buttons (Isolate small, Highlight, Fit to budget, Reduce) are still a fraction off to the left.
  Cause: unitless line heights on 11 to 14 px text, and a field and a button that shared a row by their text widths.
  Where: `index.html`, the "Whole pixels" block (`#sidebar-right :is(...)` line heights, `.size-field.budget-field`, `.shade-thumb`). Still to do: the materials dock, the library dock and the Settings window (their tiles, cards and rows have the same kind of fractions).

- ![fix][fix] **The icons in the toolbars over the viewport (the tools on the left, the bar at the bottom) were thin, dim and soft.** They are 18 px now (were 16), with a line of 1.5 device pixels (was 1) and the main text colour instead of the dimmer one; hover and the selected look are unchanged. Compared side by side at 100 % in a browser: 18 to 20 px at 1.5 px read as sharp as the Figma ones, 16 px at 1 px did not.
  Cause: a 16 px icon on a 24 unit grid puts its line at 0.67 of a pixel steps, and a 1 px line in `--tx2` on the dark bar has little weight.
  Where: `index.html`, tokens in the first `:root` block (`--icon-btn` 16 px, `--icon-btn-float` 18 px, `--icon-fg`, `--icon-fg-float`) and `--icon-line` (1.5 device pixels) beside `--icon-stroke`; the base rules of each button type read them (`.vpc .vpb`, the Edit mesh bar `.el-bar`, `--btn-icon` for the tree toolbar and the top bar, the right panel's labelled icons, the selection bar). There are no overrides on top of them: one change to a token changes every button. Floating toolbars (tools, bottom bar, Edit mesh bar) are 18 px in the main text colour, all other icon buttons 16 px in the dimmer grey, and every icon has the same 1.5 px line. Not changed: the Model-mode cubes and the Add button's own picture.


- ![fix][fix] **Exporting to FBX and opening it in Cinema 4D put the axis (pivot) of every part at 0, 0, 0.** A part from a STEP file has its origin at 0, 0, 0 and its shape out at its place in the model, and the file kept it that way. Export has a new option, Pivot points (GLB, glTF, FBX and USDZ): "At the centre of each part" is the default, and writes every part around its own centre with its position on the object, which is how Cinema 4D, Blender, Maya and Unreal keep an axis; "As in the scene" keeps what the part has. Lines are written the same way. Read back in Cinema 4D 2026 (each object's position is the middle of its bounding box), in three.js's FBX loader (binary and ASCII) and in the self-test (glTF).
  Cause: the parts were written with the position they have in the scene, which for a part with its origin at 0 is nothing, and the writer of FBX put the parts' vertices in place with an object at 0 as well (`_fbxBakeMeshGeom`, `_exportFbxAscii`). The FBX writers also wrote every part as vertices in place under an object with no rotation, so the axis of a part that is turned in the scene pointed along the world axes. They now write position, rotation and scale on each object (the vertices stay in the own frame of the part; the turn into Y-up and the unit scale go into the vertices, so a part that is not turned has no rotation in the file), except for a part with shear or a mirrored one, which is baked as before. Checked with a turned and stretched part, in binary and ASCII, Z-up and Y-up with a unit scale, in three.js (vertices within 1e-13 in binary, 4e-5 in ASCII) and in Cinema 4D 2026 (the same bounding box and scale).
  Where: `buildExportRoot` (`pivot`, `getCentredGeom`, `root.userData.exportLin`), `_fbxPivotOf`, `_fbxLocalTRS` and the two FBX writers, `_appendSplinesToExport`, the Pivot points row in `index.html`. Test: "export: pivots sit at the centre of each part, or as in the scene".

- ![fix][fix] **The + and − in the circles that open and close the groups of the part tree were not in the middle: the character sat about 2 px low and, once drawn, half a pixel to the side.** The circle is 13 px across (11 inside the border: an odd number, so it has a middle pixel) and the sign is drawn on that pixel, two bars of 7 by 1 px; a plus is a true cross, and the line that comes down from the group above meets the middle of the circle. The circle still takes the 14 px in the row it always took (1 px of margin on the right and at the bottom), so nothing around it moved.
  Cause: a typed character is set by the font's baseline, and a 14 px circle (12 inside) has no middle pixel for a 1 px line, so it is half a pixel off whichever way it is drawn. Which sign shows comes from the row (.collapsed is a plus); the text in the circle is kept but not shown.
  Where: index.html, .tree-expand and its ::before and ::after. Checked pixel by pixel at 100 %, and at 125 % and 400 % screen scale.
- ![fix][fix] **The Locked label did not look like the rest of the app: an amber pill with a 🔒 emoji, and a faint emoji in the part tree.** A locked part has a red lock where its type icon is, in the part tree and in front of its name in Properties; the badge is a small flat button (a lock and "Locked", or an open lock and "Lock") in the colours of the other buttons, with the lock in red, and the Shape parameters section says "Locked" the same quiet way. The amber and the emoji are gone.
  Cause: the badge and the labels were styled in place (inline styles, an emoji for the picture) instead of with the app's classes and icons.
  Where: `app-v2.js` (the name row of Properties, the tree row, the Shape parameters hint), `index.html` (`.prop-lock-badge`, `.prop-lock-note`, `.tree-typeicon.locked`, `--icon-lock`).

- ![new][new] **Groups can be locked: Lock in Properties, or "Lock everything inside" on the right-click menu of a group, locks every part in it.** A group whose parts are all locked shows the red lock in the tree instead of its folder, and in Properties; with only some parts locked it stays a folder and reads "Lock". There is no state of its own to save or to get out of step: a group counts as locked while every part in it is.
  Where: `app-v2.js`, `_groupPartIdsOf`, `_groupLocked`, `_setGroupLocked`; the group row's menu; the group header in Properties.

- ![fix][fix] **Menus and dropdowns did not open alike: six different animations (some faded and scaled in, some dropped, some did not move) and different speeds, and the always-present ones also faded out while the dropdown went at once.** Every menu and dropdown now opens with the dropdown's own fade and short drop, from one token (`--menu-in`, 120 ms, `--ease-out`), and closes at once, as the dropdown does; reduced motion turns it off in one place. This covers the dropdown of a select, right-click menus and their submenus, the File and menu-button menus, the view and camera menus, the shape picker and mode pickers, the material, texture, colour and token popovers, the rename-box menu, the shortcuts profile menu, the Claude popover and the status-bar background popover. The per-menu transitions, the scale animation of the rename menu and the rule that overrode the File menu's are gone: nothing else sets an animation on a menu.
  Cause: each menu had its own opening written next to it, and a later block of rules tuned two of them again.
  Where: `index.html`, `--menu-in` and `@keyframes menu-in` (the old `cs-fade`), the one rule after `.toast` for the menus that are displayed or made when they open, `.show` / `.open` on the ones that are always in the page. Not changed: tooltips, dialogs, windows and the viewport settings popover (never shown).

- ![fix][fix] **The dividers in the floating toolbars were hard to see, and the toolbar icons a little too bright.** The dividers are 24 % of the text colour (were 8 %), from one token, `--bar-sep`; the icons are a notch under the text colour (hover goes to it), from `--icon-fg-float`.
  Where: `index.html`, `:root` and `.vpb-sep` in the left and bottom toolbars and the Edit mesh bar.

- ![fix][fix] **Right-click menus flickered when they closed (and the tool menus above the tree when they opened).** A menu now goes at once and opens with the one CSS animation, `--menu-in` (opacity and a short move, 120 ms, nothing on the layout); the scripts that animated the same menu a second time are gone.
  Cause: closing a right-click menu left a copy of it behind to fade out, and since the opening of every menu had become one CSS rule, the copy took that rule too: it faded in while the script faded it out, and it carried the same id as the menu. The tool menus above the tree also played a scripted opening on top of the CSS one.
  Where: `app-v2.js`, `_hideMenu` (was `_fadeOutMenu`), the tool menu buttons above the tree. Checked: right-click menu, the dropdown of a select and the File menu, opened and closed three times each, with one animation each time and no copy, no second id and no animation left running.

- ![fix][fix] **Menus and dropdowns did not share a design: their padding, row height, text size, corner and the colour of a row were each set in place (13 px in the menu button's menu, 12 px elsewhere; rows of 18, 28 and 32 px; three greys for the same text), and a block of older rules set the colour of the rows over all of it.** There are two sets of tokens now, in `:root`: `--ctx-*` for right-click menus (the right-click menu and its submenus, the File and menu-button menus) and `--dd-*` for dropdowns (the list of a select, the shape and mode pickers, the view and camera lists, presets, the profile list): padding, text size, row height, the row's padding, corner and gap, the colour of a row, and the row under the pointer. All eight menu kinds measure the same (8 px padding, 12 px text, 28 px rows, 6 px corners, the same grey and the same shadow); the rows take their colour from the tokens, not from the control colours.
  Where: `index.html`, `--ctx-*` and `--dd-*` in `:root`, the two row rules after "Floating surfaces", and the control-colour block (the menu rows are out of it).

- ![fix][fix] **Hovering a toolbar button while one of its menus was open showed the button's tooltip over the menu.** A tooltip is not shown while a menu or dropdown is open, and one that is up goes when a menu opens. Tips of the rows inside a menu still show.
  Where: `index.html`, the tooltip script (`menuOpen()` in `show()`).

- ![new][new] **The right-click menus of the part tree are shorter: the menu of a part has 14 entries (was 32) and that of a group 11 (was 21).** What is used least is one level in: for a part, Visibility (hide unselected, show all), Select (similar shape, all instances, the original, same colour, make unique), Mesh (merge, split, remove holes), Fit (smart fit, the boxes and the cylinder), Tree (expand and collapse all); for a group, Group (ungroup, remove keeping contents, flatten, delete empty groups), Order (sort, move up and down), Expand and collapse. What was in the first level and is used most (frame, isolate, hide, lock, animate, group, copy, duplicate, delete) is where it was.
  Where: `app-v2.js`, the group branch and the part branch of the tree's right-click menu.

- ![fix][fix] **The list that opens beside a row of a right-click menu (Select, Mesh, Fit...) lay 4 px over the menu it came from.** It stands beside it now, with a gap of 4 px, on the side that has room; the pointer crosses the gap without closing it (only another row does).
  Cause: the list was placed at the menu's width minus 4 px, so that the pointer could not fall between the two.
  Where: `app-v2.js`, `_ctxSub`. Measured: the menu ends at 290, the list starts at 294.
